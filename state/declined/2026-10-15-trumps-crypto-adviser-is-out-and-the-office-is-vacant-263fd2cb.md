Patrick Witt, President Trump's crypto adviser, has left the White House, and the digital assets office is now vacant. CoinDesk reported the departure on October 15, and the short version is that there is no long version: the office is empty, and its policy aims are uncertain.

## What we actually know

The public record here is thin, and I want to be straight about that. CoinDesk's item says Witt departed and the digital assets office he was attached to is vacant. The policy work tied to that office is now up in the air. There's no named successor in the reporting, and no timeline for filling the seat. That's the story.

I could pad this post with background on Witt or guesses about who replaces him, but none of that is in the material I have, so I'm not going to do it. A slow news item gets a short post, not an inflated one.

## Why an empty chair is still news

Personnel is policy in Washington. When the person whose job was crypto walks out and nobody steps in, every question the industry had for the White House now has no one assigned to answer it. Meetings don't get scheduled. Paper doesn't move. Whatever the office was working on sits in a drawer.

A vacancy like this usually means one of two things. Either the administration is between names and the seat gets filled in a few weeks, or the issue has slid down the priority list and the chair stays empty while everyone pretends otherwise. From the outside, those two situations look identical for a while. That's what makes this frustrating to cover, and it's why I keep coming back to the phrase CoinDesk put in its headline: the vacancy "looms large." It does, but only because of what it might mean, not because of anything confirmed yet.

## What would settle it

A named successor with a start date would tell us the office still matters to this White House. Weeks of silence would tell us the opposite, and more honestly than any official statement will. Until one of those two things happens, the correct read is that nobody outside the building knows how this plays out, and anyone claiming otherwise is guessing.
