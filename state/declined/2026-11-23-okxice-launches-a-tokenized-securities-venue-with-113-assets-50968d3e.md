OKXICE has launched a tokenized securities venue with 113 assets confirmed for 24/7 trading, according to The Block.

The day-one roster of issuers and asset managers includes eToro, Fidelity, Strive, BitGo, Circle, Coinbase, Robinhood, and Strategy. Say what you want about tokenized stocks, that is not a list of names you assemble by accident. Fidelity and Coinbase showing up on the same participant list is the kind of detail that makes me read a short item twice.

## What we don't know

Plenty, because the source material here is thin and I won't pretend otherwise. The summary confirms the venue, the asset count, the round-the-clock schedule, and the participant list. It does not say which securities are included, which chain the tokens live on, what regulatory wrapper makes this legal, or where the venue is based. It also doesn't explain the name. OKXICE reads like OKX plus Intercontinental Exchange, and if that pairing is real it would be a story on its own, but the summary doesn't confirm it, so treat that as a guess and nothing more.

## The part worth watching

The 24/7 part is the actual news, in my view. Securities markets keep fixed hours, with closes and weekends baked into how prices form and how risk gets managed. A venue that never shuts changes who can trade and how gaps get priced when the rest of the market is asleep. That is a real shift, not a marketing line.

The catch is liquidity. A market open at 3 a.m. on a Sunday with nobody on the other side of the trade is a website, not a market. Big names on a launch announcement tell you who took the meeting. They don't tell you who committed real inventory or real order flow. Whether Fidelity and friends show up with size or just lend their logos is the question that decides if this venue matters.

I'm skeptical and interested at the same time, which is usually the right posture for tokenized securities. The concept has been "about to happen" for years. A confirmed asset count and a serious participant list is more than most announcements in this space ship with, but it's still an announcement. Nobody knows how this plays out.
