# Jensen Huang resigns as Nvidia CEO, and bitcoin drops with the stock

**Date:** 2026-11-12

Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia's CEO on Thursday, giving up the president's title and his board seat too, effective immediately, after a week of reports about extramarital affairs, including one with a woman U.S. intelligence agencies identified as passing information to Beijing. Shares of the world's second‑largest public company fell as much as **16%** before recovering to close down **4.3%**, according to [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-resigns-reports-extramarital-relationships-rcna608402). Bitcoin fell with them.

## The week that did it

* **Sunday:** Nvidia's board announced an independent investigation into whether Huang had been targeted by a foreign‑intelligence effort (Cointelegraph).
* **Tuesday:** The board's independent directors hired former acting U.S. Attorney **Joon H. Kim** and the law firm **Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz** to run the inquiry (Cointelegraph).
* **Wednesday:** Reports said the board was preparing to remove Huang, and NBC reported the board met that night with the White House aware.
* **Thursday morning:** Huang was gone.

## The underlying story

Huang admitted an affair with **Alice Huang** (no relation), a former executive at Singapore‑based **Megaspeed**. The *New York Times* reported that U.S. intelligence agencies concluded she was passing information to Beijing. Megaspeed bought advanced Nvidia chips and remains under a U.S. criminal investigation over allegations it smuggled American‑made chips into China (NBC).

Later on Wednesday, the *Times*, citing five current or former Nvidia employees, reported a second affair with a married healthcare executive who reported to Huang.

Huang's memo to employees, per NBC, said: 
> "I was unfaithful to my wife. I am deeply sorry."

China has denied the allegations, calling them **"purely unsubstantiated and directed at smearing China."**

## The numbers

* Nvidia's market cap rose from roughly **$57.5 billion** at the end of 2016 to a **$5.5 trillion** peak on Sept. 1, 2026 - a nearly 96‑fold increase (Cointelegraph, citing Stock Analysis).
* The stock is down **more than 26 %** over the past month and about **11 %** this week alone, a slide NBC values at **$500 billion** in erased market cap.
* Huang's net worth fell from **$162.2 billion** on Nov 9 to **$140.6 billion** by Thursday afternoon (Cointelegraph).
* **Colette Kress**, CFO since 2013, is interim CEO and retains the CFO title while the board evaluates candidates.
* **Harvey Jones**, former CEO of Synopsys, becomes Nvidia's first chairman - a governance shift for a company that ran on Huang alone for three decades (NBC).
* Kress wrote to staff: *"I believe deeply in this company and in its future."* (NBC)
* Nvidia is scheduled to report its fiscal 2027 third‑quarter results on **Nov 17** (Cointelegraph).

## Why bitcoin fell too

Cointelegraph reports bitcoin dropped as much as **3.5 %** to a multimonth low alongside Nvidia shares. CoinDesk notes that bitcoin miners expanding into AI data centers also declined, and that the AI sector often acts as a proxy for the crypto market.

The honest read is that a big chunk of the money in both trades is the same money, chasing the same AI narrative, and it heads for the exit at the first crack in the story. If you hold bitcoin because you think it trades on its own logic, Thursday was a bad day for that thesis.

## The loose ends

* **Congressional scrutiny:** Rep. **John Moolenaar** (House Select Committee on China) said lawmakers still want to question Huang and are prepared to subpoena him (Cointelegraph citing Politico). A Nov 4 letter from Moolenaar and two other committee chairs asked Huang about Beijing's access to sensitive U.S. technology and his lobbying on chip‑export restrictions.
* Rep. **Gregory Meeks** (Foreign Affairs Committee) is considering a subcommittee investigation when the new Congress convenes in January (Cointelegraph).
* **President Trump** weighed in, suggesting Apple executive chair **Tim Cook** should take the role: *"A Legend replaces a Legend!"* Cook became Apple's executive chair on Sept 1 and shows no sign of leaving soon (NBC).
