Ledger is investigating wallet drains involving buyers from CryptoBilis, and the estimated losses top $86 million, [The Block reports](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-10-09-ledger-cryptobilis-fund-losses-418163).

That is nearly everything I can tell you. The full report sat behind a security check when I pulled it, so what I have is the headline, the number, and the date. The rest is questions, and I am not going to pad them into a longer post.

## What we know

The Block's headline says the estimate "tops $86 million in losses" and that Ledger is investigating. The story ran on October 9. That is the whole confirmed picture right now.

What I don't know: what CryptoBilis is, how the drains happened, how many wallets were hit, whether the devices themselves were compromised or the buyers were, and what Ledger has said beyond the fact of the investigation. Any post that fills in those blanks today is guessing.

## My read

Eighty-six million dollars is not a footnote. If the estimate holds, a lot of people lost serious money, and the thread connecting them is a seller, not random bad luck. That points at the pipeline around the device: the purchase, the delivery, the setup, the emails that come after. In stories like this, the wallet itself is rarely the weak part. Everything wrapped around it is.

I want to be clear that the previous paragraph is inference from one headline, not reporting. Treat it accordingly.

If you bought a Ledger through CryptoBilis, I would not wait for the final report. Move your funds to a fresh wallet with a new seed, and be suspicious of any email or message that references your purchase.

Ledger's investigation will fill in the blanks eventually. Until then, the honest summary is short: a big number, a named seller, and no confirmed cause. I will come back to this when there is more than a headline to work with.
