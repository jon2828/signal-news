# Bitcoin sentiment is near rock bottom, and even the bulls are sitting on their hands
*Date: 2026-11-22*

Bitcoin's market mood appears unusually bleak despite a modest price rally. CoinDesk's weekend market roundup quoted crypto veterans saying **"this time actually seems different"** as Bitcoin slipped, suggesting that even seasoned participants are hesitant to jump in.

---

## Prices vs. sentiment

The same CoinDesk snapshot shows:

- **Bitcoin:** $60,541.89 (+2.25% today)
- **CD20 index:** $1,621.44 (+2.21%)
- **Ethereum:** $1,698.12 (+2.50%)
- **Solana:** $70.86 (+2.71%)
- **Tron:** $0.30 (‑1.33%)

Almost every major crypto is in the green, yet the quoted sentiment is decidedly bearish. This mismatch could mean the rally is a short‑term relief bounce that market participants don't trust, or that sentiment indicators are lagging the price action.

---

## Why the gloom?

The article highlights two non‑traditional concerns that are now entering the conversation about Bitcoin:

1. **AI‑related capital flows** - The broader market narrative is that AI‑driven investment may be pulling capital away from risk assets, including crypto.
2. **Quantum‑computing risk** - A separate CoinDesk piece notes that policymakers are beginning to discuss quantum‑computing threats to cryptographic systems, which adds a new layer of uncertainty for Bitcoin holders.

Both points are mentioned in the same market wrap, but the piece does not provide a detailed mechanism linking them to Bitcoin demand. Their presence alone, however, signals a shift in the risk landscape that investors are watching.

---

## Related headlines that add context

- **Gold's haven status "has lost its edge over Bitcoin, and the Fed's to blame."**  
  A CoinDesk analysis compares risk‑adjusted returns of gold and Bitcoin, suggesting that Bitcoin may be reclaiming its store‑of‑value narrative.
- **Czech National Bank remains silent on the value of its Bitcoin holdings** - A year after its purchase, the central bank still will not disclose the current worth of its crypto assets.
- **State of Crypto newsletter teases pending oversight hearings** - The newsletter hints at upcoming regulatory developments, though details are still scarce.

---

## What this could mean

When sentiment hits rock bottom while prices are modestly higher, history shows two possible outcomes:

1. **Capitulation may already be complete**, leaving the market in a quiet, exhausted state.
2. **Investors could be waiting for another leg down** before committing capital again.

The current discourse, focusing on AI and quantum‑computing risks rather than traditional crypto‑specific issues, suggests that broader technological and macro‑policy concerns are now part of the narrative surrounding Bitcoin.
