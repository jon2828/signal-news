# Trump administration reportedly plans to name Nvidia, OpenAI and additional firms as AI national champions

*Date: 2026-11-16*

The Trump administration plans to designate more than two dozen artificial‑intelligence companies as "national champions," the Wall Street Journal reported, with Nvidia, Amazon, Microsoft, OpenAI and Anthropic among those named, according to CoinDesk's coverage of the story. The stated goal is to help these firms expand overseas and compete with global rivals.

The report cites people familiar with the matter; nothing has been confirmed, and CoinDesk said it could not independently verify the story. What, if any, benefits the designation would provide remains unclear.

---

## A label in search of a policy

"National champion" is the kind of label governments sometimes use when they want to favor certain firms, CoinDesk notes. In past cases such designations have been linked to subsidies, export assistance, government contracts, or diplomatic support, but no specific measures have been disclosed for this proposed list.

The list itself – more than two dozen companies – suggests a broad approach rather than a single "champion." Whether the label will translate into concrete policy remains to be seen.

---

## The timing is the interesting part

The report arrives as the AI trade has been under pressure:

- The iShares A.I. Innovation and Tech Active ETF (BAI), with about **$11.7 billion** in assets, closed Friday at **$36.47**, down **33 %** from its June record high of **$54.46**. The slide has been especially steep since Oct. 6, when it traded above **$50**.
- The VanEck Semiconductor ETF (SMH), the largest U.S. chip‑focused fund with roughly **$65 billion** in assets, closed Friday at **$486.75**, about **28 %** below its 52‑week high of **$671.83**.
- Nvidia, the bellwether of the AI trade, closed Friday at **$162.85**, roughly **33 %** below its Oct. 6 peak of **$243.37**.

The government's move comes at a moment when the market is pulling back on AI‑related equities. One could read this as Washington trying to shore up strategic assets that appear to be on sale, or as a signal that the state is picking favorites to stabilize a wobbling sector.

---

## What to watch

- Whether the White House confirms any of these designations.
- If the "national champion" label is accompanied by concrete support such as faster export approvals or protection against foreign regulation.

Until there is an official announcement, the story remains a list of names and a label reported by a single outlet.
